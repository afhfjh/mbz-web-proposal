How it works

Four steps.
One point of contact.

You hand us the keys and the title. We handle the pickup, the paperwork, the loading and the sailing, and you watch it happen from your phone.

Pickup

About MBZ

A Miami
forwarder since 2008.

Small enough to answer your call. Licensed and experienced enough to move your cargo right.

MBZ Miami USA Corp. is a licensed ocean transportation intermediary based in Miami Gardens, Florida. For more than fifteen years we have exported vehicles, trucks, motorcycles, parts and general cargo from South Florida, with a strong focus on Latin America.

Our clients are dealers, importers and families who need a vehicle to arrive complete, with its papers in order. We treat each unit as if it were our own: photographed on arrival, secured by our own crew, and followed until it is released at destination.
